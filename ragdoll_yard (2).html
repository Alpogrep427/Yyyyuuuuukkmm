Ragdoll Yard
Host over HTTPS, open in Quest Browser, tap Enter VR.
Desktop: click to capture mouse, WASD, Space, E grab, click fire, R eject mag, Tab menu. VR: Left Y menu. Right trigger: click. Grip: grab. Trigger: fire. A/X: eject mag, then push a fresh one into the gun. Gun models: drop a Sketchfab .zip or .glb on this page (desktop), or put pistol.glb, smg.glb, shotgun.glb, rifle.glb in a models folder beside this file (add ?flip=1 if barrels point backwards). Weapon models from Sketchfab: credit each author as its license requires. Left stick: move. Right stick: turn, press to jump.
